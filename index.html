Formulas — fit: total×bytes + KV/1K×context + 2 GB overhead. Decode t/s: bandwidth ÷ (active×bytes) × 50%. Prefill t/s: TFLOPS × 35% ÷ (2×active). Break-even: price×(1−resale) ÷ (t/s × 3600 × h/day × hosted $/M × 365/10⁶). Gates per card: fit → decode ≥ 5 t/s → TTFT ≤ 30 min (warn > 5) → break-even ≤ 3 y = BUY. Resale is a uniform 40% assumption on every card — one year of a 3–4 y corporate amortization horizon, not measured (a real 12-mo Mac trade-in returned 11%).
